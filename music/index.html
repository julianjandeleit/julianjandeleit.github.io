Music

I play piano and guitar and like to play-along, improvise and draft new melodies

Original Works

Some piano improvisations have turned into their own pieces. Finished pieces, where I have written sheet music for are:

  • Perfectly Superficial
  • Spiral Waves

Perfectly Superficial, was originally inspired by the Black Mirror episode Nosedive.

Piano

I took over 12 years of piano lessons in my childhood, receiving training in both classical and modern music. Now, I enjoy playing along with pop and rock songs, as well as performing neoclassical minimalist pieces. I’ve also taught myself to improvise in this style, which has led to a few distinct compositions.

Some pieces i like to play:

  • Canon in D, Pachelbel
  • Passacaille, Händel
  • Winter I, Vivaldi
  • November, Wolfgang Fuchs
  • Tu Sei, Ludovico Einaudi
  • Porz Goret, Yann Tieren
  • All of Me, Jon Schmidt

Guitar

At some point, I started picking up the guitar more and more often, have now been taking lessons since 2024 to improve my skills. Here, I focus on styles that are new to me, especially bossa nova, blues, jazz. Of course, I’ve also tried my hand at rock and for example transcribed “Sometimes I Feel Like Screaming” by Deep Purple.

Some pieces i like to play:

  • Manhã de Carnaval, Luiz Bonfá
  • Blue Bossa, Kenny Dorham
  • Bossa-Nove Breeze & Etude, Gerald Schwertberger
  • Etude No. 6, Leo Brouwer
  • Nothing Else Matters, Metallica
  • Sometimes I Feel Like Screaming, Deep Purple
