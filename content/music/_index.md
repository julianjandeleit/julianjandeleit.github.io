_Perfectly Superficial_, was originally inspired by the Black Mirror episode _Nosedive_.

# Piano

I took over 12 years of piano lessons in my childhood, receiving training in both classical and modern music. Now, I enjoy playing along with pop and rock songs, as well as performing neoclassical minimalist pieces. 
I've also taught myself to improvise in this style, which has led to a few distinct compositions.

Some pieces i like to play:
 -  _Canon in D_, Pachelbel
 - _Passacaille_, Händel
 - _Winter I_, Vivaldi
 - _November_, Wolfgang Fuchs
 - _Tu Sei_, Ludovico Einaudi
 - _Porz Goret_, Yann Tieren
 - _All of Me_, Jon Schmidt

# Guitar

At some point, I started picking up the guitar more and more often, have now been taking lessons since 2024 to improve my skills. Here, I focus on styles that are new to me, especially bossa nova, blues, jazz. Of course, I've also tried my hand at rock and for example transcribed “Sometimes I Feel Like Screaming” by Deep Purple. 

Some pieces i like to play:
 - _Manhã de Carnaval_, Luiz Bonfá
 - _Blue Bossa_, Kenny Dorham
 - _Bossa-Nove Breeze & Etude_, Gerald Schwertberger
 - _Etude No. 6_, Leo Brouwer
 - _Nothing Else Matters_, Metallica
 - _Sometimes I Feel Like Screaming_, Deep Purple
